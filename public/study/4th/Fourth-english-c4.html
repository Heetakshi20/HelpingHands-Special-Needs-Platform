Elements of Creative Writing
  • 👉 Characters: Who is in your story?
  • 👉 Setting: Where and when does it happen?
  • 👉 Plot: What happens (beginning, middle, end)?
  • 👉 Conflict: What problem do characters face?
  • 👉 Theme: What is the message or lesson?
