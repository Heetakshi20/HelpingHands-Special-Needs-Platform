Main Idea and Supporting Details
  • 👉 Main Idea: What the passage is mostly about (the big picture)
  • 👉 Supporting Details: Facts, examples, and evidence that explain the main idea
  • 👉 Think of the main idea as the roof of a house, and details as the walls that hold it up
The Amazing Honeybee

Honeybees are fascinating insects that play a vital role in our ecosystem. These hardworking creatures spend their days collecting nectar from flowers, which they transform into delicious honey. As they move from flower to flower, they transfer pollen, helping plants to grow fruits and seeds. A single honeybee colony can contain up to 60,000 bees, each with a specific job. Worker bees gather food, nurse bees care for the young, and the queen bee lays eggs. Unfortunately, honeybee populations have been declining due to pesticides, loss of habitat, and disease. Scientists and farmers are working together to protect these essential insects because without them, many of our favorite foods would disappear.
