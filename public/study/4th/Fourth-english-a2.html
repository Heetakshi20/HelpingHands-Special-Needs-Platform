Drawing Conclusions
  • 👉 A conclusion is a smart guess based on evidence
  • 👉 Combine text clues + what you already know = conclusion
  • 👉 Authors don't always tell you everything directly

Background Knowledge

Conclusion
The Missing Cookies

Maria had baked a fresh batch of chocolate chip cookies for the school bake sale. She left them on the kitchen counter to cool while she went to answer the phone. When she returned five minutes later, the plate was nearly empty. Only three cookies remained. Max, the family's golden retriever, was lying under the kitchen table with crumbs on his nose. He wagged his tail happily and licked his lips. Chocolate chips were scattered on the floor near his paws.

🔍 Clues from the text:
⏱️ The cookies were left on the counter for five minutes
🍪 Only three cookies remained when Maria returned
🐕 Max had crumbs on his nose and chocolate chips near his paws
What can you conclude about what happened to the cookies?
