The Five-Paragraph Essay
  • 👉 Introduction: Hook + Background + Thesis Statement (main idea)
  • 👉 Body Paragraph 1: First main point with evidence and examples
  • 👉 Body Paragraph 2: Second main point with evidence and examples
  • 👉 Body Paragraph 3: Third main point with evidence and examples
  • 👉 Conclusion: Restate thesis + Summarize points + Final thought
