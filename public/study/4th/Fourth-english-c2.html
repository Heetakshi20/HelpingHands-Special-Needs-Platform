"Learn how to research and cite your sources."
Research Process
  • 👉 Ask: What do I want to learn? Form a research question.
  • 👉 Find: Look for reliable sources (books, websites, encyclopedias).
  • 👉 Record: Take notes and write down where you found information.
  • 👉 Cite: Give credit to your sources to avoid plagiarism.
