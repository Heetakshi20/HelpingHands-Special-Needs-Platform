Complex Sentences
  • 👉 Independent clause: Can stand alone (I went to the store.)
  • 👉 Dependent clause: Cannot stand alone (Because it was raining...)
  • 👉 Subordinating conjunctions: because, when, if, although, since
Independent Clause
I went to the store.
The sun was shining.
Dependent Clause
Because it was raining
When I got home
I stayed inside
it was raining
