"Build a paragraph using the hamburger model."
The Hamburger Paragraph
  • 👉 Top Bun: Topic sentence (what the paragraph is about)
  • 👉 Patty & Toppings: Detail sentences (facts, examples)
  • 👉 Bottom Bun: Concluding sentence (wraps it up)

Concluding Sentence
Topic Sentence
Dogs are wonderful pets.
Detail
They are loyal and love to play.
Detail
Dogs can learn tricks like sit and stay.
Detail
They make great companions for walks.
Concluding Sentence
That's why dogs are the best pets.
