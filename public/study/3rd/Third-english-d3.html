"Make the subject and verb agree."
Subject-Verb Agreement
  • 👉 Singular subject → singular verb (add -s)
  • 👉 Plural subject → plural verb (no -s)
  • 👉 Example: The dog runs. / The dogs run.
Singular
The cat runs
She plays
Plural
The cats run
They play
The dog in the park.
