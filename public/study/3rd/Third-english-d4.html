D4 · Quotation Marks

"Use quotation marks to show someone's exact words."
Quotation Marks Rules
  • 👉 Use quotation marks before and after someone's exact words
  • 👉 Capitalize the first word of the quote
  • 👉 Put punctuation inside the quotation marks
  • 👉 Start a new paragraph when the speaker changes
✅ Correct
"Time for dinner!" Mom said.
"I love pizza," said Tom.
❌ Incorrect
Mom said, Time for dinner! (missing quotes)
I love pizza, said Tom. (missing quotes)
Sarah asked, Are we there yet?
Sarah asked , Are we there yet ?
