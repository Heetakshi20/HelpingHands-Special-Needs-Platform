A3 · Making Inferences

"Read between the lines to make an inference."
What is an Inference?
  • 👉 An inference is a smart guess using clues from the text
  • 👉 Combine what you read + what you know = inference
  • 👉 Authors don't always tell us everything directly
Text Clues
+
What You Know
=
Inference
🧥 Sarah put on her warmest coat and boots.
💨 She could see her breath in the air.
❄️ Snow was falling outside her window.
What can you infer about the weather?
