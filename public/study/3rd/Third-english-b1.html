"Identify the parts of a story's structure."
Story Structure (Plot Mountain)
  • 👉 Exposition: Beginning - introduces characters and setting
  • 👉 Rising Action: Events that lead to the problem
  • 👉 Climax: The most exciting part, turning point
  • 👉 Falling Action: Events after the climax
  • 👉 Resolution: How the story ends

The Lost Kitten

Lily was playing in the park when she heard a tiny meow. Under a bush, she found a small gray kitten shivering and alone. Lily wanted to help the kitten find its home. She asked everyone in the park if they knew who the kitten belonged to, but no one did. Lily made posters with a picture of the kitten and put them up around the neighborhood. The next day, a girl named Emma saw the poster and came to Lily's house. Emma said the kitten was hers and it was named Whiskers. Lily was happy that Whiskers was going home, and Emma thanked Lily for being so kind. They became good friends and played together often.
