B3 · Setting and Plot

"Analyze where and when the story happens, and what happens."
Setting and Plot
  • 👉 Setting: where and when the story takes place
  • 👉 Plot: the sequence of events in the story
  • 👉 Setting can affect the mood and what happens in the plot
The Lost Kitten

One sunny afternoon in the park, Lily was playing on the swings when she heard a tiny meow. She looked under a bush and found a small gray kitten shivering and alone. Lily wanted to help the kitten find its home. She asked everyone in the park if they knew who the kitten belonged to, but no one did. Lily went home and made posters with a picture of the kitten. She put them up around the neighborhood. The next day, a girl named Emma saw the poster and came to Lily's house. Emma said the kitten was hers and it was named Whiskers. Lily was happy that Whiskers was going home, and Emma thanked Lily for being so kind. They became good friends and played together often.

Setting
📍 Place: the park, Lily's house, neighborhood
⏰ Time: sunny afternoon, next day
🌤️ Weather: sunny
Plot (Main Events)
1 Lily finds a lost kitten in the park
2 She asks people in the park if they know the owner
3 Lily makes and puts up posters
4 Emma sees a poster and comes to get her kitten
5 Lily and Emma become friends
