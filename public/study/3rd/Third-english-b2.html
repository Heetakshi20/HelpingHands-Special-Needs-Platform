Character Traits
  • 👉 Traits are words that describe a character's personality
  • 👉 Look at what characters SAY, DO, and THINK
  • 👉 Use evidence from the text to support your ideas
👧 Lily
👂 Lily heard a tiny meow and stopped to look for the kitten.
🗣️ She asked everyone in the park if they knew who the kitten belonged to.
📋 Lily made posters and put them up around the neighborhood.
