The Amazing Honeybee

Honeybees are fascinating insects that play a vital role in our world. They pollinate flowers, which helps plants grow. A single honeybee can visit up to 5,000 flowers in one day! Bees make honey from nectar, and a hive can produce up to 60 pounds of honey in a year. There are three types of bees in a hive: the queen, workers, and drones. Without bees, we would have much less food to eat.
